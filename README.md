## The entry

One number, digits only: `83`.

No words, no explanation, no screenshots. The count is the whole answer.

## Why 83

Eighty-three tabs is a number a real person could have open right now: several windows, a mix of work docs, shopping carts, half-read articles and videos paused mid-sentence. It is high enough to surprise, specific enough to read as counted rather than guessed, and it is not one of the numbers everyone reaches for. The obvious picks are round (100, 500, 1000), joke numbers (69, 420, 404), or 47 — which the internet has already claimed as the official tab-hoarder count, with its own meme pages and a whole listicle genre. 83 dodges all three traps.

## How it is built

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the number to `entry.txt` and validates it first: 1 to 5 characters, digits only. The file holds exactly `83`, two bytes, no trailing newline.
